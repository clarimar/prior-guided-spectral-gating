HIGHLIGHTS

- Prior-guided gating layer integrates chemometric importance measures 
  (ANOVA/VIP/RF) with deep learning for interpretable NIR spectroscopy

- KL-divergence regularization maintains statistical grounding while 
  allowing data-driven refinement (correlation 0.35-0.51 with priors)

- Lightweight architecture (1.7k-6.9k params) achieves R²=0.86-0.93, 
  competitive with PLS while avoiding catastrophic overparameterization

- Learned gates identify chemically relevant bands (931nm C-H for fat), 
  validated across food and petrochemical domains

- Framework designed for small-sample regime (n=50-500) typical of 
  analytical chemistry applications
