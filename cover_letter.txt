Dear Editor,

We submit for your consideration our manuscript entitled "Prior-Guided 
Spectral Gating for Interpretable Deep Learning in Near-Infrared 
Spectroscopy" for publication in Chemometrics and Intelligent Laboratory 
Systems.

This work addresses a critical gap in chemometric practice: enabling deep 
learning adoption without sacrificing the interpretability that makes 
traditional methods like PLS the gold standard. Our framework introduces 
three technical innovations:

1. Initialization from established chemometric importance measures 
   (ANOVA, VIP, Random Forest)
2. KL-divergence regularization maintaining statistical grounding
3. Lightweight architecture appropriate for small-sample regimes (n=50-500)

Validation on two benchmark NIR datasets (Tecator and Gasoline) demonstrates 
competitive performance with PLS (R²=0.86-0.93 vs 0.92-0.94) while providing 
explicit, chemically validated band importance maps. The top selected band 
for Tecator (931nm) corresponds precisely to C-H absorption for fat content.

We believe this work fits perfectly within the scope of Chemometrics and 
Intelligent Laboratory Systems, offering a practical pathway for 
chemometricians to leverage deep learning while maintaining interpretability.

All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to 
Chemometrics and Intelligent Laboratory Systems.
